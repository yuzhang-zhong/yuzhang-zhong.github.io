This project draws on theories from NLP and linguistics to study how
AI-introduced new entities influence meaning-making and the creative process in
human-AI collaborative writing. I am interested in how new characters, objects,
settings, or concepts generated by AI reshape human imagination, planning,
ownership, and narrative direction.

## Research Question

How do AI-generated new entities change the dynamics of human creativity during
collaborative writing?
