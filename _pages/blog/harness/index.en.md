I am studying how agent harnesses differ across domains and which mechanisms might transfer between them. This series collects my reading notes and comparisons.

## Topics

1. [Coding harnesses](/blog/harness/coding/en/): the inner loop that completes a task and the outer loop that improves the harness.
2. [Auto-research harnesses](/blog/harness/auto-research/en/): workflows for literature review, idea selection, experiments, and paper writing.
3. [Harnesses for exploratory data analysis](/blog/harness/eda/en/): organizing an analysis to turn data and a broad question into useful insights.
4. [Quantitative finance harnesses](/blog/harness/quantitative-finance/en/): the evidence boundaries between executable research, historical simulation, and live trading.
5. [Coding RSI harnesses](/blog/harness/coding-rsi/en/): how an agent's process for proposing and testing its next version can improve.
